*Fighting in Fizi Displaces Thousands as Humanitarian Crisis Deepens
South Kivu, February 4, 2026 — Intense fighting between the FARDC and its allies and AFC/M23 rebels has forced thousands of civilians to flee in Fizi, leaving many without water, food, or healthcare. Authorities estimate up to one million people have been displaced across the territory.
Displaced families are sheltering with host communities, while children are out of school, malnutrition is rising, and access to medical care remains severely limited. The ICRC is responding, but the crisis continues to worsen amid ongoing violence.
*IMF Warns Security Spending Is Derailing DRC Budget Targets
Kinshasa, February 4, 2026 — The International Monetary Fund (IMF) says security spending is the main cause of budget deviations in the Democratic Republic of Congo, following its second program review.
The IMF reports that prolonged fighting in eastern DRC led to an increase in security expenditures of about 1.2% of GDP, forcing repeated adjustments to fiscal targets and reducing funds for social and investment programs.
Two key targets were missed: limits on emergency spending and minimum levels of social spending. While the IMF approved a higher deficit ceiling due to security pressures, it warned that continued reliance on emergency procedures threatens fiscal discipline and remains the program’s primary vulnerability.
*MONUSCO Boosts Ceasefire Monitoring Support Between DRC and AFC/M23
Kinshasa, February 4, 2026 — The UN Stabilization Mission in the DRC (MONUSCO) announced it has strengthened its preparedness to support the ceasefire monitoring and verification mechanism between the Congolese government and the AFC/M23, following talks facilitated in Doha by Qatar.
MONUSCO confirmed it is authorized under UN Security Council Resolution 2808 (2025) to assist in implementing a permanent ceasefire, and will provide technical and logistical support through the ICGLR-backed mechanism (EJVM+).
The Mission is expected to deploy an initial team to Uvira in the coming days to monitor compliance, as parties reaffirmed their commitment to the Doha Peace Framework signed on November 15, 2025.
*Clashes Resume Between FARDC and CRP in Bule, Djugu Territory
Ituri Province, February 4, 2026 — Violent fighting erupted between the FARDC and the Convention for the Popular Revolution (CRP) militia led by Thomas Lubanga in Bule, Djugu territory, after a brief lull.
The clashes, which began around 8:00 a.m., lasted nearly six hours, triggering panic, forcing civilians to flee again, and paralyzing socio-economic activities.
The renewed violence comes just days after a joint FARDC–MONUSCO mission aimed at reassuring residents. No official casualty figures have yet been released.
*Four Civilians Killed in ADF Ambush Near Oicha, North Kivu
Mulolya, North Kivu, February 3, 2026 — Four civilians were killed and six motorcycles burned in an ambush by ADF rebels on the Oicha–Mulolya–Mamove road, about 10 km west of Oicha, the main town of Beni territory.
The attack occurred around 8:00 a.m., with rebels coming from west of Mamove, which was itself attacked two days earlier. This brings the death toll in the area to nine civilians in three days.
Another ambush was reported in Malibo the same day, though targeted farmers escaped to Oicha. Traffic remains suspended, and victims’ bodies were taken to the Oicha General Hospital morgue.
The FARDC Deputy Chief of Staff is currently assessing the security situation in Beni.
